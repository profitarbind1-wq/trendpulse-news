Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to TrendPulse 360 (accessible from https://trendpulse-live.web.app). One of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by TrendPulse 360 and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

TrendPulse 360 does not require users to create an account or provide personal credentials to read news. We may collect anonymous browser logs including IP addresses, browser types, internet service provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, and operating systems for analytical purposes.

2. Cookies and Web Storage

We use standard local storage to store user preferences such as Dark Mode toggles and Saved Articles/Bookmarks. These settings remain stored locally on your device.

3. Third-Party Advertisements & Google AdSense

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on TrendPulse 360. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site.

4. Content Syndication & Attribution

TrendPulse 360 aggregates real-time news headlines from verified public press feeds (RSS). Full credit and links to original publishers (e.g. BBC, Reuters, Google News) are prominently attributed on every article.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us via our Contact Page.
